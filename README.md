<p align="center">A Slay The Spire 2 character mod which adds <b>The Tailor</b>, a stylish clothier with a score to settle.</p>

<hr>

<img src="https://github.com/hex3gc/TheTailor/blob/main/TheTailor/images/showcase/0_selectScreen.png?raw=true">

The character starts with:
- 5 **Strikes**
- 4 **Defends**
- 1 **Sew**
- 1 **Craft**

<img src="https://github.com/hex3gc/TheTailor/blob/main/TheTailor/images/showcase/2_startingDeck.png?raw=true">

### Card Stitching
The Tailor can **Stitch** his cards together - when one is played, the other is played afterward for free. This allows him to circumvent Energy costs, set up combos, and pull cards from different piles to narrow his deck down to the essentials.

<img src="https://github.com/hex3gc/TheTailor/blob/main/TheTailor/images/showcase/3_stitching.png?raw=true">

### Minions
Certain cards allow you to create **Minions**, living fabric creatures who take damage for you and have a variety of abilties depending on their make. You can change their order, sacrifice them, and combine different types to make the most of their effects.

<img src="https://github.com/hex3gc/TheTailor/blob/main/TheTailor/images/showcase/4_minions.png?raw=true">

### Upgrades
The Tailor has an **Upgrade** archetype with various ways to enhance your cards during combat. **Premium** cards can be Upgraded any number of times, allowing you to spend your campfires strengthening your strongest card while enhancing the rest during combat.

<img src="https://github.com/hex3gc/TheTailor/blob/main/TheTailor/images/showcase/5_premium.png?raw=true">

### Custom graphics
All assets have been made with Megacrit's style in mind to make The Tailor feel like he fits among the rest of the cast. **No AI was used in the making of this mod.**

<img src="https://github.com/hex3gc/TheTailor/blob/main/TheTailor/images/showcase/6_cardArt.png?raw=true">

Many cards and assets still have missing or placeholder graphics which will be replaced in the future.
